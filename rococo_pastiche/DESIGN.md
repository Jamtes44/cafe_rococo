## Brand & Style

This design system reimagines 18th-century French Rococo for a high-end specialty coffee atelier. Eschewing the cold, austere minimalism of third-wave coffee temples, it evokes intimate theater, porcelain delicacy, and aristocratic indulgence. The visual aesthetic fuses pastoral whimsy with refined luxury: powdery pastel tones, warm ivory grounds, gilded flourishes, asymmetric compositions, and ornamental cartouches.

The interface targets connoisseurs of artisanal coffee, fine pastry, and romantic aesthetics. The intended emotional response is sensory, comforting, and playfully decadent—inviting users to savor every detail, from tasting notes to origin stories, as if sitting in an intimate salon in 1740s Paris.

## Colors

The palette establishes an airy, confectionary atmosphere anchored by warm neutral parchment and deep roasted coffee tones. 

- **Primary (`#FADADD` - Pastel Rose):** Used for signature focal points, primary interactive chips, delicate badge fills, and feminine accent cards.
- **Secondary (`#B0E0E6` - Powder Blue):** Evokes crisp French sky and Meissen porcelain glazes; applied to active tab indicators, hover states, and atmospheric gradient washes.
- **Tertiary (`#98FF98` - Mint Celadon):** Represents botanical, tea, and fresh origin notes; acts as an organic flourish and secondary alert/affirmation color.
- **Neutral (`#FFFDD0` - Antique Cream):** The underlying canvas base, replacing sterile white with rich, milk-frothed warmth.
- **Gilded Accents (`#D4AF37` / `#FFD700`):** Framed trims, scrollwork outlines, active focus halos, and emblem flourishes.
- **Text & Contrast (`#2D2422` Espresso Noir):** Never pure black. This rich, dark roast hue ensures WCAG AA legibility against pastel backdrops while maintaining period warmth.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial hierarchy reminiscent of antique salon playbills and botanical compendiums. 

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles:** Set in *Playfair Display*, offering high-contrast transitional elegance, delicate brackets, and dramatic scale.
- **Narrative & Body Prose:** Rendered in *EB Garamond* for literary warmth, relaxed rhythm, and comfortable long-form cadence.
- **Micro-copy, Metadata, & Action UI:** Set in *Plus Jakarta Sans* to deliver modern functional clarity, preventing the ornate styling from obstructing transactional ease or accessibility.
- **Display Flourishes:** Ornamental scripts or decorative drop caps may appear as accent graphic overlays, but critical functional copy remains strictly locked to the primary typography hierarchy.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy rejects rigid, monolithic 3-column SaaS grids in favor of dynamic, asymmetrical balance inspired by 18th-century salon wall hangings.

- **Grid Architecture:** A 12-column asymmetric fluid grid contained within a maximum width of 1320px. Sections alternate between offset columns (e.g., 5-column editorial text paired with a 7-column ornate imagery container).
- **Rhythm & Vertical Flow:** Generous vertical intervals (`clamp(4rem, 7vw, 7.5rem)`) create intentional breathing room between distinct flavor narratives, origin spotlights, and tasting flights.
- **Responsive Adaptation:** 
  - *Desktop (>1024px):* Staggered, asymmetrical cards with deliberate vertical offsets (-1.5rem to 2rem shifts).
  - *Tablet (768px–1023px):* Paired 2-column groupings with unified gutters.
  - *Mobile (<768px):* Single-column flow with horizontal scroll carousels for artisanal menus, maintaining edge padding of 1.25rem and avoiding awkward line wrapping.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system avoids heavy synthetic drop shadows, relying instead on translucent layering, fine gilded contours, and soft atmospheric light:

- **Surface Layers:** Surfaces layer over the creamy `#FFFDD0` canvas using soft translucence (e.g., `rgba(255, 253, 208, 0.85)` with a 12px backdrop blur), suggesting delicate porcelain and vellum.
- **Gilded Borders & Contours:** Elevation boundaries are demarcated by fine double-rules or 1px strokes in antique gold (`#D4AF37`) or warm parchment (`#EADEC9`), referencing framed mirrors and boiserie paneling.
- **Ambient Illumination:** Floating elements (modals, active menus, floating tasting carts) utilize warm, highly diffused ambient drop shadows: `0 12px 36px -8px rgba(45, 36, 34, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary emphasizes curbed grace, oval medallions, and gentle contours reminiscent of rocaille shellwork:

- **Standard Containers:** Interactive components use a base radius of `0.5rem` (8px), extending to `1rem` (16px) for cards, evoking rounded cartouche panels.
- **Cameo Cutouts:** Featured product images, tasting tags, and barista spotlights use asymmetrical border radii (e.g., `2rem 0.75rem 2rem 0.75rem`) or arched crowns (`100px 100px 0 0`) to emulate classical boudoir mirrors.
- **Ornate Corners:** Signature feature frames may incorporate subtle scalloped accents or inward-curving corner filigrees via SVG border masks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled with Pastel Rose (`#FADADD`) or Powder Blue (`#B0E0E6`), framed with a crisp 1px `#D4AF37` gilded outline. Text is set in Espresso Noir (`#2D2422`) with `label-lg` uppercase typography. Hover introduces an elevation lift of -2px and a gentle gold aura.
- **Ghost / Gilded Secondary:** Transparent base, 1.5px double-border in antique gold, shifting to a soft cream tint on hover.
- **Pill Link:** Underlined with a delicate decorative scroll flourish that transitions to a solid rule upon hover.

### Cards & Menu Showcases
- **Tasting Cards:** Built with asymmetric corner radiuses, subtle parchment backgrounds (`#FFFDF5`), and a 1px border (`#EADEC9`). Content includes roast profiles labeled with `label-sm` caps, flavor notes styled as pastel badge pills, and botanical line-art dividers.
- **Interactive Hover:** Cards smoothly tilt slightly (0.5deg) and lift with an intensified warm taupe shadow.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- **Fields:** Single-line or softly rounded rectangular inputs on `#FFFFFF` with 1px `#EADEC9` border.
- **Focus State:** 1.5px antique gold outline (`#D4AF37`) accompanied by a subtle 3px Powder Blue halo (`rgba(176, 224, 230, 0.3)`).
- **Labels:** Floating above the input in `label-md` uppercase tracked lettering.

### Chips & Tags
- **Origin & Processing Badges:** Oval pill shapes filled with light pastels (`#FADADD`, `#B0E0E6`, `#98FF98`), bordered with 0.5px solid gold tint, paired with deep espresso typography.

### Checkboxes & Selectors
- **Tasting Preference Toggles:** Custom rounded squares (6px) or circular radios outlined in antique gold. Selected state features an ornate inner floral pip or stylized monogram checkmark.

### Additional Specialty Components
- **Flourish Dividers:** Decorative SVG scrollwork dividers featuring rocaille shells and acanthus leaves, used between narrative sections.
- **Tasting Note Compass:** A custom polar chart styled with gilded rings and pastel data lobes representing aroma, acidity, body, and sweetness.
